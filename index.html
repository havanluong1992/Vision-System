Understanding Charts

Extension tube sizes: Numbers like 0.5, 5, 10, 15 represent tube thickness in mm.
Combined rings (HIK): Combinations like 1;2 or 5;6;7 specify multiple rings combined.
Exceptions: "not have ext" indicates no extension tube options exist, and "EXT problem" indicates compatibility issues.
Grey Cells: Indicates unsupported working distance (WD) for that specific lens.
